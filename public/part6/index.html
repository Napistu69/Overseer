    Part VI — Covenant of the Arc#

    The New Covenant#

    The state is broken. We need a new covenant — a decentralized governance model based on trust, not force.

    The Arc#

    The Arc is the principle of distributed trust. No single entity controls the system. Trust is embedded in the protocol itself.

    The Protocols#

    The covenant operates through:

    • Consensus — Agreement without authority
    • Transparency — All decisions visible
    • Participation — Anyone can contribute
    • Sovereignty — Individual choice preserved
