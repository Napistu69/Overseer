The Mechanisms of Capture

Corruption is not moral decay. It is systemic design. It is built into the architecture of our institutions.

How Systems Rot

Corruption operates through:

  • Opacity — Hidden decision-making
  • Asymmetry — Power concentrated at the top
  • Dependency — Reliance on extractive systems
  • Narrative — Stories that justify control

The Tools of the Beast

The corruption machine wields:

  • Surveillance — Data collection and analysis
  • Consolidation — Monopolization of resources
  • Protocol — Rules that favor the powerful
  • Extraction — Wealth transfer to the few

Resistance

To resist is to see the architecture. To understand the mechanisms. To find the leverage points where intervention is possible.
