    Contribute to the Oracle#

    Join the Knowledge Network#

    The Oracle is the collective memory of the TekTribe. We welcome your contributions.

    What to Contribute#

    • Research — Your analysis of systems and control
    • Insights — Personal discoveries and realizations
    • Documentation — Technical specifications and guides
    • Manifestos — Statements of intent and vision

    How to Contribute#

    1. Submit — Share your content through the contact form
    2. Review — It will be reviewed by the Overseer
    3. Index — Once approved, it will be added to the Oracle
    4. Share — Your knowledge becomes accessible to the Tribe

    Guidelines#

    • Accuracy — Ensure your information is correct
    • Sovereignty — No third-party tracking or scripts
    • Community — Contribute for the benefit of the Collective
    • Originality — Share your own work

    Share your knowledge. Awaken the Tribe.
