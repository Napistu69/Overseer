    Part V — The Rise of Goliath#

    The Path to Monopoly#

    Goliath is not a conspiracy. It is inevitable. When systems consolidate, they inevitably create a single controlling entity.

    The Mechanisms#

    Consolidation operates through:

    • Acquisition — Buying competitors
    • Patents — Blocking alternatives
    • Regulation — Favoring the powerful
    • Infrastructure Control — Owning the rails

    The Result#

    A single entity controls:

    • Data — All information flows through Goliath
    • Infrastructure — The platforms we rely on
    • Narrative — The stories we consume
    • Behavior — How we act and think

    Resistance#

    To resist Goliath is to:

    • Decentralize — Distribute control
    • Open Source — Share the technology
    • Cooperate — Build alternatives together
    • Awaken — See the consolidation happening
