## The Architecture of Watching

Surveillance is not a tool. It is the **architecture** of the modern state. It is built into our infrastructure, our devices, and our daily lives.

---

## Data as Weapon

Every click, every purchase, every movement is collected, analyzed, and weaponized. Data becomes:

- **Predictive** — Anticipating behavior
- **Control** — Enforcing compliance
- **Extraction** — Monetizing attention
- **Surveillance** — Watching without consent

---

## Privacy as Myth

Privacy no longer exists. It is a **myth** sold to keep us compliant. The state sees all. The corporations know everything.

---

## Resistance

To resist surveillance is to:

- **Encrypt** — Protect communications
- **Anonymize** — Hide identity
- **Distribute** — Decentralize data
- **Awaken** — Understand what is being watched
