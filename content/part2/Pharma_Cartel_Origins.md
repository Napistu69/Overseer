## Pharma Cartel Origins - The Profit-from-Poison Paradigm

As the web evolves, so must health data. Blockchain-based medical records allow individuals to own their genomic and biomarker information. Decentralized Science (DeSci) platforms fund independent research outside the NIH/Big Pharma loop. Tokenization incentivizes contribution to public datasets rather than proprietary silos.

These are not futuristic dreams. They are occurring now. The Hydra reacts violently—lawsuits, shutdowns, lobbying. But the spine cannot adapt fast enough to kill every decentralized node. Every time a protocol succeeds outside the gate, the myth of centralized necessity weakens.

The Third Reich Transplant was successful for seventy years. But empires rot from the inside when the root starves. And the root is now starving.
