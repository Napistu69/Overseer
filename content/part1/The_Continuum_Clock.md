## The Continuum Clock

The Petrol Goliath believes the clock is broken, that time has run out. They believe they are the only ones who know how to turn the handle. But they have forgotten that the handle is in the hands of every awakened Sol.

The Clock is an hourglass. When it runs dry, it flips. Sometimes, if the sand is sticky with corruption, it flips early and chaotically. But if we cleanse the sand, if we restore the Mykes, we can flip it smoothly, preserving the essence of the previous age while seeding the next.

We are not victims of the Winds. We are the Windmasters. The dial is in our hands. Turn it.
